Theatrical Indie Showcase

Overview:

To put my JavaScript skills to the test, I decided to recreate the War card game entirely from scratch using nothing but the three core web technologies. I've included additional features to make the game more interesting and allow the player to do more than simply draw card after card from the deck they choose at the start of the game. I took inspiration from "Mario's Game Gallery" (1995) when making the dynamic text bar at the top of the screen.

Features:

  • A fully playable reimagining of the War card game including a brand new set of rules as explained on the Rules page.
