Theatrical Indie Showcase

Overview:

I needed to make a web application with login and sign-up pages with Google Authenication, so I decided to make this website. At the time I've created it, there had been five successful indie animated films and four live action independent hits. Any films that came shortly after such as "The Backrooms" are not included as I made this back in late April 2026. I couldn't leave out "Obsession", however, due its incredible profit margin.

Features:

  • Includes functional login and sign-up pages with the option for the user to sign in with their Google account.
  • Features ten independent productions that were notably successful in theaters between 2024 to 2027.
  • Each production has its own page including box office information, achievements, the trailer, and links to where users can watch it if they're interested.
